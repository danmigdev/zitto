zitto for the Mac (experimental)

It needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer), macOS 14 or newer, 7 GB free on the disk.

1. Drag zitto onto Applications.

2. Open it from there. This app is not signed with a paid Apple certificate, so the first time macOS
   says that it could not verify it. Choose Done, then:
       System Settings > Privacy & Security > at the bottom, "Open Anyway"
   On macOS 14 you can also right-click zitto, then Open.

3. The first time it downloads what it needs, about 3.5 GB: Python, the libraries, ffmpeg and the
   separation models. Then it opens by itself. If you quit in the middle, the next time it goes on.

Use it with recordings that you made, or that you have the right to use in this way. The terms of three
of the four models are not known: see https://github.com/danmigdev/zitto#how-it-works

To remove it: delete zitto from Applications, and in the Finder, Go > Go to Folder, delete
    ~/Library/Application Support/zitto
which also holds the songs separated so far.

If you try it, please say how it went: https://github.com/danmigdev/zitto/issues
