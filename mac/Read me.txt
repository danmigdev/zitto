zitto for the Mac

It needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer), macOS 14 or newer, 5.5 GB free on the disk (3.5 without
the optional models).

1. Drag zitto onto Applications.

2. Open it from there. This app is not signed with a paid Apple certificate, so the first time macOS
   says that it could not verify it. Choose Done, then:
       System Settings > Privacy & Security > at the bottom, "Open Anyway"
   On macOS 14 you can also right-click zitto, then Open.

3. The first time it asks which separation models to have, and downloads them with Python, the
   libraries and ffmpeg: from 1.1 to 3.6 GB. Then it opens by itself. If you quit in the middle, the
   next time it goes on. To add or remove models later: Settings, in the zitto menu.

Use it with recordings that you made, or that you have the right to use in this way. The terms of four
of the five models are not known: see https://github.com/danmigdev/zitto#how-it-works

To remove it: delete zitto from Applications, and in the Finder, Go > Go to Folder, delete
    ~/Library/Application Support/zitto
which also holds the songs separated so far.

If something is not right: https://github.com/danmigdev/zitto/issues
